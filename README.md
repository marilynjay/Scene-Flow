# Scene Flow

A compact, collapsible outliner for planning a novel scene by scene.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's nothing to install or build.

## What it does

- **Nested sections** like *Arrival day → Cottage scenes*, as deep as you like. Each one collapses. Alt-click a section's arrow to fold or unfold everything inside it.
- **Scene cards** with three levels: the title row, a short description below it, and a **Details** area below that for beats, dialogue and research. With the description folded, the title row shows a one-line preview.
- **POV, place, cast and threads** show as chips inside an opened scene, above its description. Tap a chip to change it, or type a new name to add one on the spot.
- **POV button** (top bar): shows each scene's POV character at the end of its row, in their colour (a dash when there isn't one), for spotting whose head you've been in lately. Tap a name to change it; tap POV again to hide them.
- **Place tags**: a scene with a place shows that place's short label (CHAR, Field, Hall…) at the start of its row; tap it to change the place. Set a place's short label in its profile.
- **Name highlights**: characters marked “Highlight name in scene titles” in their profile (the CHAR Unit, to start) get their colour behind their name wherever it appears in a scene title. The cast and thread labels open on who or what is already in the scene (tap a name for their profile, ✕ to remove), with **Add characters** / **Add plot threads** for the full list.
- **Time markers** between scenes show as a slim line (label, month, and the temperature range once a month is set); tap one to edit it, and its arrow folds it back. (e.g. "1 month into classes" + Oct). Pick a month and the marker shows that month's typical weather from your **Climate by month** table. Type your own weather to override it.
- **Library** (left rail): characters and places. Click a character to open their profile: title, age, group, track, height, hair, eyes (with an eye-colour picker), other appearance details, power, power growth (start → end), personality, backstory, relationships and other notes. Empty fields are hidden when viewing; **Edit** shows every field with a hint, plus rename and delete. Places have a name, colour and description. Each row shows a scene count, and a filter button that highlights every scene they appear in.
- **Intensity heatmap**: click the coloured edge on the left of a scene to rate it 1–5, from Calm (blue) through Uneasy (green), Tense (yellow) and Intense (orange) to Critical (red). The **Heatmap** button cycles colours + *Intensity flow* chart → colours only → off; click a bar in the chart to jump to that scene.
- **Plot threads**: name your threads in the side list (or type a new one from a scene's thread label) and tag scenes with as many as apply. 
- **Filter** (top bar): pick any character, place or plot thread. **Show only** hides everything else (including sections with no matching scenes); **Dim others** keeps the whole outline visible and fades what doesn't match.
- **To-dos & questions**: give any scene a checklist (⋯ → Add to-do or question, or + To-do or question under its description). Items ending in “?” become questions; tap an item's icon to switch. Scenes with open items show a badge, and the **To-dos** button lists everything. **Changes across scenes** are one to-do tagged on many scenes (“Move the first day to Monday”): pick scenes by searching their text, tick each scene off as you revise it, watch the progress, and tap **Show** to filter the outline to the scenes still left.
- **Status** on each scene: planned, drafted or done. On computers it’s the dot beside the title; on phones it’s in the scene’s ⋯ menu (phones also skip drag handles and scene numbers to leave room for titles).
- **Tap a section's name** to step its scenes and sub-sections through Folded → Sub-sections (sections that have them) → Titles → Descriptions → Everything (section descriptions stay as they are; use the notes icon beside the name for those). Rename a section from its ⋯ menu (or double-click its name on a computer).
- **Section descriptions**: give any section a short description (+ Description on its heading, or ⋯ → Add description) and optional Details. The description stays visible under the heading even when the section is folded.
- **View** buttons along the top set every section and scene at once; after that, open or fold anything individually with its arrow (and show or hide a section's description with the notes icon beside its name). The buttons: **Headings** (section titles only), **Sections** (every section and its description, with scenes hidden), **Titles** (section and scene titles only), **Descriptions** (section and scene descriptions) and **Everything** (descriptions and details, for sections and scenes).
- **Drag** the ⠿ handle to reorder. Drop onto a section heading to move a scene into it. The ⋯ menu has Move up/down, **Move to…** (pick any section, works on phones and tablets), Move to holding, Duplicate, Delete and Add below.
- **Scene editor**: ⋯ → Edit scene (or tap a scene's title on a phone) opens a full panel: title; compact Status, Intensity, Place and POV buttons that pop up their choices; cast and plot threads as chips; then description, details and to-dos. ‹ Prev / Next › move through scenes in story order, and **+ New scene after** adds the next scene (in the same place) and keeps you in the editor. One Undo reverses a whole editing session.
- **Turn into section** (scene ⋯ menu): when one scene turns out to be several, it becomes a section with the same name and description, and the original scene (with its place, cast, tags and to-dos) becomes the first scene inside, ready to rename.
- **Holding** sits below the outline for scenes you've set aside but might use later. Held scenes aren't numbered, counted or shown in the intensity flow. Move them back with Move to… or by dragging.
- **Share with AI** turns your outline into plain text with names written out, ready to paste into an AI chat. You can leave out scene details, status or the weather table, or, while a filter is on, share only the matching scenes.
- **Search** (`/`) and **Undo** (Ctrl/⌘+Z) for structural changes.

## Starting your own book

The app opens with an example book. Click **Start my own book** in the banner at the top, or **Backup → Start a new, empty book** at any time. Loading your own file with **Backup → Open file… → Replace outline with this** also replaces the example.

## Updates

When a new version is published, the app shows **A new version of Scene Flow is ready → Reload**. Your outline is saved first.

## Saving to your phone's home screen

Open the site in Safari (iPhone/iPad) and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, or in Chrome (Android) tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen**. It opens full screen with its own icon.

On iPhone and iPad the home-screen app keeps its own saved copy, separate from Safari. To move an outline you started in Safari, use **Backup → Copy** there, then **Backup**, paste, and **Replace** in the home-screen app.

## Saving

Everything saves automatically to your browser's local storage on this device. Use **Backup** to copy or download your outline as a file now and then, and to restore it on another device. **Add from file** in the same window adds what a file has that you don't: characters, places and plot threads not in your lists yet (matched by name), plus its sections and scenes at the end of your outline and its typical weather for any months you haven't filled in. Nothing you already have is changed or removed. Scenes already in your book (same title, description and details) are skipped, and it tells you first. **Remove duplicate scenes**, also in Backup, cleans up a book that already has copies, keeping the first of each.
