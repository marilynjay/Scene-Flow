# Scene Flow

A compact, collapsible outliner for planning a novel scene by scene.

Open `index.html` in any modern browser. There's nothing to install or build.

## What it does

- **Nested sections** like *Arrival day → Cottage scenes*, as deep as you like. Each one collapses. Alt-click a section's arrow to fold or unfold everything inside it.
- **Scene cards** with three levels: the title row, a short description below it, and a **Details** area below that for beats, dialogue and research. With the description folded, the title row shows a one-line preview.
- **Plaques** on the right of each scene: characters present, place, and POV. Click one to choose from your library, or type a new name to add it on the spot.
- **Time markers** between scenes (e.g. "1 month into classes" + Oct). Pick a month and the marker shows that month's typical weather from your **Climate by month** table. Type your own weather to override it.
- **Library** (left rail): characters and places. Click one to open its details window, where you can change its name, colour and notes, or delete it. Each row shows a scene count, and a filter button that highlights every scene they appear in.
- **Intensity heatmap**: click the coloured edge on the left of a scene to rate it 1–5, from Calm (blue) through Uneasy (green), Tense (yellow) and Intense (orange) to Critical (red). The **Heatmap** button turns the colours and the *Intensity flow* chart on or off; click a bar in the chart to jump to that scene.
- **Status dot** on each scene: planned, drafted or done.
- **Show** buttons expand the whole outline to Sections, Scenes, Summaries or Details.
- **Drag** the ⠿ handle to reorder. Drop onto a section heading to move a scene into it. The ⋯ menu has Move up/down, **Move to…** (pick any section, works on phones and tablets), Move to holding, Duplicate, Delete and Add below.
- **Holding** sits below the outline for scenes you've set aside but might use later. Held scenes aren't numbered, counted or shown in the intensity flow. Move them back with Move to… or by dragging.
- **Share with AI** turns your outline into plain text with names written out, ready to paste into an AI chat. You can leave out scene details, status or the weather table.
- **Search** (`/`) and **Undo** (Ctrl/⌘+Z) for structural changes.

## Starting your own book

The app opens with an example book. Click **Start my own book** in the banner at the top, or **Backup → Start a new, empty book** at any time. Loading your own file with **Backup → Open file… → Replace outline with this** also replaces the example.

## Saving to your phone's home screen

Open the site in Safari (iPhone/iPad) and tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, or in Chrome (Android) tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen**. It opens full screen with its own icon.

On iPhone and iPad the home-screen app keeps its own saved copy, separate from Safari. To move an outline you started in Safari, use **Backup → Copy** there, then **Backup**, paste, and **Replace** in the home-screen app.

## Saving

Everything saves automatically to your browser's local storage on this device. Use **Backup** to copy or download your outline as a file now and then, and to restore it on another device. **Add new characters & places** in the same window brings in only the characters and places from a file that aren't in your list yet, leaving your scenes and existing notes alone.
